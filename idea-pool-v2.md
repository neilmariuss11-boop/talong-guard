# Idea Pool v2 — "Wow + Useful + Money + Local"
**2026-09-12 — Passion-filtered, not ABE-filtered**

---

### 1. LoRa Mesh Emergency Network ("TyphoonNet")
ESP32 + LoRa modules form mesh network that works WITHOUT cell signal. During typhoons when towers go down, barangays can still send distress/status messages between nodes. Each node ~₱800 (ESP32 + LoRa SX1276). Solar powered.
- **Wow:** Mesh networking, works when everything else dies
- **Money:** Sell to LGUs via DRRM funds, NGOs, Red Cross partnerships
- **Local:** Mindoro gets hit by typhoons. Cell towers fail. People die because no comms.

### 2. Smart Tricycle Fare Meter
Arduino + GPS + small OLED screen. Tracks distance, calculates fair fare, displays to passenger. Ends fare disputes. 3D-printed enclosure. Data logs routes for city planning.
- **Wow:** Turns every tricycle into a "Grab" with transparent pricing
- **Money:** Sell device to tricycle operators (₱500-1k unit). LGU could mandate it.
- **Local:** Tricycle fare disputes are daily life in every Philippine town

### 3. Offline-First Community Marketplace App ("TindahanKo")
Mobile app that works on crappy internet. Sync when connected, browse offline. For buying/selling within your municipality. Not Shopee — hyperlocal, works in Mindoro's connectivity reality.
- **Wow:** Offline-first architecture. Works where Shopee/Lazada don't.
- **Money:** Transaction fees, promoted listings, delivery partnership
- **Local:** Rural towns have stuff to sell but no digital marketplace that works for them

### 4. Livestock GPS Tracker + Anti-Theft Alert
ESP32 + GPS + LoRa. Collar for carabao/cattle. Tracks location, alerts owner via SMS if animal leaves geofence. Carabao theft is a real crime in rural PH.
- **Wow:** GPS geofencing, LoRa long-range, anti-theft for a ₱50-100k animal
- **Money:** Sell tracker (₱1-2k) for animal worth ₱50-100k. Easy sell.
- **Local:** Carabao theft reported regularly in Mindoro provinces

### 5. Portable Water Quality Tester ("SafeWater")
ESP32 + TDS sensor + pH sensor + turbidity sensor. Tests drinking water in 30 seconds. Phone app logs results per water source, builds community water quality map.
- **Wow:** Citizen science meets public health. Community data mapping.
- **Money:** Sell device to LGUs (rural health units), schools, NGOs. Service: water testing per household.
- **Local:** Many Mindoro barangays on deep wells/springs with unknown quality

### 6. Solar-Powered Phone Charging Station (Vending Style)
Solar panel + battery bank + coin-operated charging slots. Put in public markets, waiting areas, terminals. Pay ₱5-10 for a charge.
- **Wow:** Passive income machine. Sustainable energy angle.
- **Money:** Direct coin revenue + billboard ad space on the station
- **Local:** Power reliability varies, people need phone charge, vendors already exist but not solar/automated

### 7. Barangay Digital Services Kiosk
Raspberry Pi + touchscreen + thermal printer. Self-service: print barangay clearance, cedula, blotter request. Reduces lines and "office hours only" problem. Works offline, syncs later.
- **Wow:** Digital government at barangay level. "Why doesn't this exist?"
- **Money:** Per-transaction fee (₱10-20 per document) or LGU subscription
- **Local:** Barangay hall bureaucracy is universal PH pain

### 8. Fish Catch Weight + Price Logger ("DagitApp")
Bluetooth scale + phone app. Fishermen weigh catch at shore, app logs weight + species + price. Over time: tracks income, proves earnings for loans, spots price trends.
- **Wow:** Financial inclusion for fishermen. Data they never had.
- **Money:** Freemium app + Bluetooth scale sales. Microfinance partnership for loan referrals.
- **Local:** Mindoro has huge fishing industry, fishermen keep zero records

### 9. Smart Parking / Traffic Counter (Municipal)
ESP32 + ultrasonic sensors at market/terminal parking. Counts available spots, displays on board or app. Also counts vehicle traffic for town planning data.
- **Wow:** "Smart city" tech for small towns
- **Money:** LGU contracts, parking management fees
- **Local:** Market parking in Calapan, Mamburao is chaos

### 10. QR-Based Jeepney/Bus Pass System
NFC/QR card + reader at jeepney/bus entrance. Tap to pay, tracked rides, auto-fare. Like Beep card but for provincial routes.
- **Wow:** Cashless transport in provinces
- **Money:** Transaction fees, data analytics for transport planning
- **Local:** Provincial transport has no digital payment system at all
