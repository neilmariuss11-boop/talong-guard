# Phase 6: Validation Plan — Autodissemination Station
**Rewritten 2026-09-27** (replaces the eggplant monitoring-trap validation plan)

> **What this is and isn't.** This is **product and customer validation** (does the device do its job, and will people pay?). It is **not** hypothesis-driven scientific research, which the team ruled out. The biology is taken from literature (`research-synthesis.md`). Our tests only check that *our* device and *our* cartridges perform, and that customers want them.

**Site:** San Jose (and neighboring Magsaysay), Occidental Mindoro
**Season:** Nov 2026 – Mar 2027 (onion planted Nov–Jan, harvested Jan/Feb–Apr [R20b])

---

## 1. FARMER INTERVIEW SCRIPT (Tagalog, with English guide)

**Target:** 20+ onion farmers in San Jose and Magsaysay. Mix: ≥5 with <1 ha, ≥5 with ≥1 ha, ≥3 women farmers, ≥3 members of an irrigators' association.
**Duration:** 20–25 minutes. **Rule:** no selling in sections A–B. Listen first. Don't mention our product until section C.
**Consent (read aloud):**
> "Kami po ay mga estudyante ng MinSU. Nagtatanong kami tungkol sa pagsisibuyas para sa aming proyekto. Kusang-loob po ito, pwede kayong tumigil anumang oras, at hindi namin ilalabas ang pangalan ninyo."
> *(We are MinSU students asking about onion farming for our project. This is voluntary, you can stop at any time, and we won't publish your name.)*

### Section A: Current problem (don't lead)
1. **"Ano po ang pinakamalaking problema ninyo sa pagsisibuyas nitong mga nakaraang taon?"** *(What have been your biggest problems growing onion in recent years?)* Record the top 3 in their words. Did they mention harabas **unprompted**? Y/N
2. **"Kailan po kayo huling tinamaan ng harabas? Gaano kalawak ang pinsala?"** *(When were you last hit by harabas? How much damage?)* Record the year, % area, and whether they harvested early.
3. **"Paano ninyo nalalaman na may harabas na?"** *(How do you know harabas is present?)* Options: see larvae / moths / trap / technician / neighbor.
4. **"Ilang beses po kayo nag-i-spray laban sa harabas sa isang season? Anong gamot?"** *(How many times do you spray against harabas per season? Which product?)* Record the number of sprays and product names.
5. **"Magkano po ang gastos sa isang spray — gamot at bayad sa tao?"** *(How much does one spray cost: chemical plus labor?)* **This is the key number for the ROI.**
6. **"Gumagana pa ba ang gamot? May napansin ba kayong humina ang epekto?"** *(Do the chemicals still work? Have you noticed them getting weaker?)* This probes resistance [R43].
7. **"May pheromone trap po ba kayo mula sa DA o munisipyo? Ano ang ginagawa ninyo sa bilang ng gamu-gamo?"** *(Do you have a pheromone trap from the DA or the municipality? What do you do with the moth count?)*
8. **"Nakagamit na po ba kayo ng Metarhizium o 'berdeng amag' / biological na pangontra?"** *(Have you used Metarhizium, "green mold" or other biological controls?)* Record the experience.

### Section B: Money and decision-making
9. **"Magkano po ang puhunan ninyo sa isang ektarya ng sibuyas?"** *(How much do you invest per hectare of onion?)* Reference: ₱200–300k [R20].
10. **"Sino po ang nagpapahiram o nagfi-finance?"** *(Who lends to or finances you?)* Trader / coop / bank / own funds.
11. **"Sino po ang nagdedesisyon kung ano ang bibilhing gamot?"** *(Who decides which inputs to buy?)* Self / spouse / trader / technician.
12. **"Saan po kayo bumibili ng gamot at abono?"** *(Where do you buy chemicals and fertilizer?)* Name the store. (Distribution channel.)

### Section C: Concept test (now show the prototype or picture)
Explain in about 30 seconds:
> "Ito po ay parang pheromone trap, pero **hindi nanghuhuli**. Ang lalaking gamu-gamo ay papasok, madadampian ng *Metarhizium* — natural na amag na pumapatay ng harabas — at lalabas. Pagkatapos, siya mismo ang magkakalat ng amag sa mga babaeng gamu-gamo at sa mga itlog at uod. Walang i-spray. Papalitan lang ang 'sleeve' tuwing 2 linggo at ang pain tuwing 4 na linggo."
> *(This is like a pheromone trap, but it **doesn't catch anything**. The male moth goes in, gets dusted with Metarhizium — a natural mold that kills harabas — and leaves. Then he spreads the mold to the female moths and to the eggs and larvae himself. Nothing to spray. You just replace the "sleeve" every 2 weeks and the lure every 4 weeks.)*

13. **"Ano po ang unang reaksyon ninyo? May tanong o duda?"** *(What's your first reaction? Any questions or doubts?)* Record objections word for word.
14. **"Ano ang kailangan ninyong makita para maniwala na gumagana ito?"** *(What would you need to see to believe it works?)*
15. **"Kung gagamitin ninyo, ilan ang kailangan ninyo para sa lupa ninyo?"** *(If you used it, how many would you need for your land?)* This tests their intuition on density.

### Section D: Price ladder
Ask in this order and record each answer:
16. "Sa anong presyo bawat Station ninyo masasabing **sobrang mura — parang hindi gagana**?" *(At what price per Station would you say it's **so cheap it probably doesn't work**?)*
17. "…**mura at sulit**?" *(…**cheap and good value**?)*
18. "…**mahal pero bibilhin pa rin**?" *(…**expensive, but you'd still buy it**?)*
19. "…**sobrang mahal, hindi na bibilhin**?" *(…**too expensive, you wouldn't buy it**?)*
20. Same four questions for the **season refill (lure + 6 sleeves)**.
21. **"Kung may demo sa kapitbahay at nakita ninyong gumana, bibili po ba kayo sa susunod na season?"** *(If a neighbor had a demo and you saw it work, would you buy next season?)* Definitely / probably / maybe / no.
22. **"Mas gusto po ba ninyo: (a) bilhin, (b) upahan bawat season kasama ang serbisyo, (c) bayaran pagkatapos ng anihan?"** *(Would you prefer to (a) buy it, (b) rent it per season with service, or (c) pay after harvest?)*

### Post-interview scoring (fill in immediately)

| Item | Score |
|---|---|
| Harabas mentioned unprompted (A1) | 0 / 1 |
| Damaged in the last 3 seasons (A2) | 0 / 1 |
| Sprays ≥3 per season against harabas (A4) | 0 / 1 |
| Reports declining chemical effect (A6) | 0 / 1 |
| Positive first reaction (C13) | 0 / 1 / 2 |
| "Expensive but would buy" ≥ ₱500 (D18) | 0 / 1 |
| Would buy after seeing a demo (D21 = definitely/probably) | 0 / 1 |
| **Total (max 8)** | |

**Strong lead:** score ≥ 6. Invite them as a demo farmer or neighbor observer.

## 5. FIELD DEMONSTRATION (V4)

### 5.1 Layout
- **3–5 demo farms** in San Jose (and 1 in Magsaysay if possible), chosen with the MAO. Each farm is ≥0.5 ha of onion transplanted Nov–Dec 2026.
- On each farm:
  - **Station block:** ≥0.5 ha with Stations at 4/ha (min 2 Stations). On at least 1 farm, also a **2/ha block** to compare density.
  - **Farmer-practice comparison:** the farmer's own practice on the rest of the farm, or a neighbor's field ≥200 m away.
- **The farmer keeps spraying when they judge it necessary.** We never ask a farmer to risk the crop. We **record** decisions; we don't restrict them.
- One **plain monitoring pheromone trap** (DA-style) per farm, ≥100 m from the Stations, to show when moths are flying (catch counts). This helps interpret results, and farmers already know it.

### 5.2 What we record (simple, farmer-visible)

| Measure | How | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Moth flight | Count moths in the plain monitoring trap | Weekly |
| Mycosed larvae ("amag" larvae) | 10-minute walk on a fixed W-shaped path in each block; count dead larvae with white or green fungal growth; photograph them | Weekly from day 21 |
| Live larvae / damage | 10 random plants per block: count larvae, score leaf damage 0–4 | Weekly |
| Sprays | Farmer log: date, product, cost | Every spray |
| Station condition | Checklist: lure present, sleeve in, damage | Every visit (2-weekly) |
| Cartridge viability | Swab 1 Station per farm at swap time | Every 2 weeks |
| Yield (optional) | Farmer's harvest record per block, if they separate it | At harvest |
| Farmer satisfaction | 5-question end-of-season survey | End |

**What we don't claim:** yield effects or statistically proven population reduction. That would need replicated research. We report what the farmers saw and did.

### 5.3 Farmer field day (mid-season, ~day 40–50 after transplanting)
- Invite: 30–50 farmers, MAO, Provincial Agriculturist, DA-RCPC, and local radio or Facebook pages.
- Show:
  - (1) How the Station works (dusted moth under a hand lens).
  - (2) Mycosed larvae collected that week.
  - (3) Spray logs so far.
  - (4) Pricing and the pre-order sheet.
- Budget: ₱10,000 (snacks, printed Tagalog flyers, tarpaulin).

### 5.4 Data collection forms

**Form A: Weekly block record**

| Farm | Block (Station 4/ha, Station 2/ha, Farmer) | Date | DAT | Trap moths/week | Mycosed larvae on path | Live larvae/10 plants | Damage score (0–4) avg | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Form B: Farmer spray log (kept by the farmer, photographed at each visit)**

| Date | Block | Product | Amount | Cost (chemical) | Labor cost | Reason (saw larvae / trap / technician / neighbor / schedule) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Form C: Station service log**

| Date | Station ID | Lure changed Y/N | Sleeve changed Y/N | Sleeve batch | Damage/issues | Swab taken Y/N | Germination % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Form D: End-of-season farmer survey (5 questions)**
1. "Nakakita po ba kayo ng uod na may amag?" *(Did you see larvae with mold?)* Y/N; how many?
2. "Mas kaunti po ba ang spray ninyo kumpara sa dati o sa kapitbahay?" *(Did you spray less than before, or than your neighbor?)* Less / same / more.
3. "Madali po bang gamitin at palitan?" *(Was it easy to use and refill?)* 1–5.
4. "Irerekomenda po ba ninyo sa kapitbahay?" *(Would you recommend it to a neighbor?)* 0–10 (Net Promoter Score).
5. "Bibili po ba kayo sa susunod na season? Ilan?" *(Will you buy next season? How many?)* Pre-order with deposit.

## 7. SUCCESS CRITERIA SUMMARY

### Must-pass (to continue to Year 2 sales)
1. V1: ≥60% of farmers rank harabas top-2; ≥40% would pay ≥₱500 per Station.
2. V2: ≥80% spore pickup; no trapped moths.
3. V3: ≥70% germination at day 14 (or a working shorter swap interval).
4. V4: mycosed larvae observed in Station blocks on ≥2 farms; zero farmer reports of harm.
5. V5: ≥10 paid pre-orders or 1 LGU letter of intent.

### Nice-to-have
- Station blocks recorded fewer sprays than farmer-practice blocks on ≥2 farms.
- NPS ≥ 30.
- A local radio or DA Facebook feature.
- An NCPC or PhilRice partner agrees to co-develop the v2 cartridge.

### Kill or pivot signals
- V2 < 50% pickup after all variants (A1–A3, C, D): **pivot the design** (e.g., electrostatic carrier, a different geometry).
- Farmers are strongly negative on price (median "too expensive" < ₱300): **pivot the business model** (LGU-only, rental or service model).
- FPA says no pathway for cartridges: **pivot to hardware + lures** and a partnership with a registrant or government biocontrol lab for spores.

## 9. ETHICAL, SAFETY AND REGULATORY NOTES
1. **Informed consent** for all interviews and demo farms (verbal, recorded in the notebook; written for demo farms).
2. **No crop risk transfer:** farmers keep full control of spraying. We never ask them to stop spraying.
3. **FPA:** before installing, confirm with FPA whether the demonstration needs an **Experimental Use Permit** (`proposal.md` §9.2). **No sale of cartridges** in the demo season.
4. **PPE:** loaders wear an N95 mask and gloves; farmers get sealed sleeves and gloves.
5. **Spore source records:** isolate, source institution, batch QC.
6. **University requirements:** check whether MinSU requires a research-ethics or extension clearance for off-campus demos; get adviser sign-off.
7. **Data privacy:** store farmer names separately from answers; comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
