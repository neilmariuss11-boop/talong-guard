**2026-09-12 | Loop Turn 5**

## 1. STRUCTURED CUSTOMER INTERVIEW SCRIPT

**Target:** 10-15 eggplant farmers in Mindoro (mix of prior contacts + new)
**Setting:** In-person at farm or barangay hall. 20-30 minutes. Filipino/local dialect.
**Bring:** Printed concept image, BOM cost sheet, sample pheromone trap photo.

---

### SECTION A: Current Problem (Confirm Pain Exists)

**Q1. Gaano kalaki ang pinsala ng uod ng talong sa ani mo noong nakaraang season?**
*(How much damage did eggplant borers cause to your harvest last season?)*
- Follow-up: What percentage was reject-grade because of borers?
- **Target:** 30%+ loss confirms problem severity.
- **Validates:** Problem is real and significant enough to pay for a solution.

**Q2. Gaano kadalas ka mag-spray ng pesticide sa isang season? Magkano ginagastos mo?**
*(How often do you spray per season? How much do you spend?)*
- Follow-up: Do you spray on schedule or when you see damage?
- **Target:** 20-80x/season, P10,000-50,000 confirms blind spraying.
- **Validates:** Current practice is expensive and wasteful.

**Q3. Paano mo nalalaman kung kailan dapat mag-spray? May senyales ka bang sinusunod?**
*(How do you know when to spray? Do you follow any signs?)*
- Follow-up: Have you heard of pheromone traps for monitoring?
- **Target:** Schedule-based or gut feel. Confirms no data-driven approach.
- **Validates:** Farmers lack monitoring tools.

---

### SECTION B: Solution Fit (Will They Use It?)

**Q4. [Show concept image] Kung may device na automatic na nagbibilang ng moth sa gabi, tapos nagpapadala ng text kung kailangan mo nang mag-spray — gagamitin mo ba?**
*(If a device automatically counted moths at night and texted you when to spray — would you use it?)*
- Follow-up: What concerns would you have?
- **Target:** Yes with concerns = good. Flat no = probe why.
- **Validates:** Farmers will act on SMS alerts.

**Q5. Kumportable ka ba sa text message bilang abiso? O mas prefer mo ibang paraan?**
*(Are you comfortable with SMS notifications? Or prefer another method?)*
- Follow-up: Phone always with you in the field? Signal?
- **Target:** SMS fine for most.
- **Validates:** SMS is right delivery channel.

**Q6. Kung natanggap mo ang text na "SPRAY TODAY" — gagawin mo ba agad?**
*(If you got a text saying "SPRAY TODAY" — would you act immediately?)*
- Follow-up: What would make you trust the device?
- **Target:** Willing to act if trust built. Probe trust-building needs.
- **Validates:** Farmers will change spraying behavior based on alerts.

---

### SECTION C: Willingness to Pay

**Q7. Kung P3,000 ang device pero makakatipid ka ng P10,000-25,000 sa pesticide kada season — bibilhin mo ba?**
*(If device costs P3,000 but saves P10,000-25,000 in pesticide per season — would you buy?)*
- Follow-up: Maximum you would pay?
- **Target:** Yes at P3,000 = strong. Note max WTP.
- **Validates:** P2,500-3,500 price point acceptable.

**Q8. Paano ka bumibili ng bagong kagamitan? Cash, installment, o sa cooperative?**
*(How do you buy new equipment? Cash, installment, or coop?)*
- Follow-up: Buy individually or through coop?
- **Target:** Reveals sales channel and financing needs.
- **Validates:** Purchase channel assumptions.

---

### SECTION D: Logistics

**Q9. May signal ba ng Globe o Smart sa taniman mo?**
*(Globe or Smart signal in your farm?)*
- Follow-up: Can you reliably send/receive texts from the field?
- **Target:** "Tolerable" or better.
- **Validates:** Cell signal available for SMS delivery.

**Q10. Kung libre ang device para subukan sa isang season, papayag ka ba? Conditions mo?**
*(Free device to test for one season — agree? Conditions?)*
- Follow-up: Would you record spraying and harvest data for us?
- **Target:** Yes = pilot participant recruited. Note conditions.
- **Validates:** Farmers will participate in pilot.

## 2. PILOT PROGRAM DESIGN

### Overview

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Design | Controlled field trial: Treatment vs Control |
| Duration | 1 full cropping season (~3-4 months) |
| Location | 2-3 barangays in Mindoro with eggplant farming |
| Participants | 6 farmers: 3 treatment + 3 control |
| Devices | 2 TalongGuard prototypes |

### Participant Selection
- Currently growing eggplant (at least 0.25 hectare)
- Experiences borer damage regularly
- Has mobile phone with SMS
- Willing to record data weekly
- Treatment and control farms similar in size, variety, location

### Groups

**Treatment (3 farmers) — "TalongGuard Farmers"**
- Receive 1 device each (or shared between nearby farms)
- Spray ONLY when SMS alert says threshold exceeded
- Record: spray dates, pesticide cost, harvest weight, reject rate

**Control (3 farmers) — "Normal Practice"**
- Farm as usual (calendar spraying or whatever they do)
- Record same data: spray dates, cost, harvest, rejects
- Receive free device AFTER trial (incentive for honest participation)

### What We Measure

| Metric | How Measured | Treatment Expected | Control Expected |
|--------|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Spray applications/season | Farmer log | 20-35 | 50-80 |
| Pesticide cost (P) | Receipts + log | P8-15k | P20-50k |
| Harvest yield (kg) | Weigh at harvest | Same or better | Baseline |
| Reject rate (%) | Count reject vs marketable | Lower | Baseline |
| Moth counts/night | Device auto-log | Recorded | N/A |
| Device accuracy | Manual vs auto count | Cross-verify weekly | N/A |
| SMS delivery success | Device log vs farmer | Track | N/A |
| Farmer satisfaction | Exit interview | 1-5 scale | N/A |

## 5. TIMELINE

### Pre-Pilot (Weeks 1-6)

| Week | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 1-2 | Conduct 10-15 customer interviews |
| 2-3 | Analyze results, confirm price/channel assumptions |
| 3-4 | Build 2 prototypes (per Phase 5 build order) |
| 5 | Lab testing: count accuracy, SMS, power cycle |
| 6 | Recruit 6 pilot farmers, sign consent forms |

### Pilot (Weeks 7-20, ~1 cropping season)

| Week | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 7 | Install devices. Train treatment farmers. Distribute forms. |
| 8-18 | Weekly visits: collect logs, verify accuracy (Form D), replace lures every 4 weeks |
| 12 | Mid-season data review, adjust threshold if needed |
| 18-20 | Harvest period: collect Form C from all 6 farmers |

### Post-Pilot (Weeks 21-23)

| Week | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 21 | Compile data: treatment vs control comparison |
| 22 | Exit interviews. Give control group free devices. |
| 23 | Write pilot report. Go/no-go decision for production. |

**Total: ~6 months from interviews to go/no-go.**

## 6. ETHICAL AND PRACTICAL NOTES

1. **Informed consent:** All farmers sign simple consent form. Experimental device. Can withdraw anytime.
2. **No harm:** Control group farms normally — no restriction on spraying. Treatment CAN spray outside alerts if they choose (we record it).
3. **Free devices for control:** Promised after trial. Ethical + incentivizes honest participation.
4. **Data privacy:** Farmer names kept confidential. Use codes (T1, T2, T3, C1, C2, C3).
5. **University ethics:** Check if MinSU requires clearance for field trials. Advisor sign-off strengthens credibility.
6. **Lure replacement:** Research team handles during pilot (not farmer burden). Track lifespan data.
