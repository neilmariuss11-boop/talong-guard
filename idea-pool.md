## Category A: Post-Harvest & Food Quality

### A1. Smart Grain Moisture Meter (Low-Cost)
**Problem:** Farmers sell rice/corn at wrong moisture → price penalties or spoilage.  
**Solution:** ESP32 + capacitive moisture sensor + mobile app. Cheaper than commercial meters (₱2-5k vs ₱8-15k commercial).  
**Revenue:** Hardware sales + calibration service.  
**ABE edge:** Post-harvest engineering is core ABE.

### A2. Solar Dryer Monitor
**Problem:** Sun-drying rice/copra is guesswork — over/under-drying wastes money.  
**Solution:** ESP32 + temp/humidity sensors inside solar dryer → alerts when target moisture reached.  
**Revenue:** Sell sensor kit to dryer owners, or as add-on for existing solar dryer designs.  
**ABE edge:** Solar dryer design is literally ABE curriculum.

### A3. Fruit Ripeness Classifier (Camera-Based)
**Problem:** Mango/banana traders misjudge ripeness → waste or premature sale.  
**Solution:** Smartphone app using camera + ML model to classify ripeness stage.  
**Revenue:** Freemium app, premium for bulk grading reports.  
**ABE edge:** Post-harvest quality assessment.

### A4. Copra Quality Grader App
**Problem:** Copra (dried coconut) quality grading is subjective → price disputes.  
**Solution:** Phone camera + ML to classify copra grade (resecada, primera, segunda).  
**Revenue:** Subscription for traders, per-assessment fee for farmers.  
**ABE edge:** Post-harvest processing.

## Category B: Farm Automation & IoT

### B1. Greenhouse Climate Controller
**Problem:** Small greenhouse farmers manually vent/water — inconsistent, labor-intensive.  
**Solution:** ESP32 + DHT22 + soil moisture sensor + relay-controlled fan/pump. ~₱2-3k BOM.  
**Revenue:** Sell kit + setup service. Recurring: sensor replacement, cloud dashboard subscription.  
**ABE edge:** Controlled environment agriculture, irrigation systems.

### B2. Automated Irrigation Scheduler
**Problem:** Smallholder farmers irrigate by schedule/gut, wasting water or stressing crops.  
**Solution:** Soil moisture sensor + ESP32 + solenoid valve. Waters only when needed.  
**Revenue:** Hardware kit sales. Target: high-value vegetable farmers.  
**ABE edge:** Irrigation engineering is core ABE.

### B3. Fish Pond Water Quality Monitor
**Problem:** Tilapia/bangus pond fish kills from low dissolved oxygen, wrong pH.  
**Solution:** ESP32 + pH sensor + DO sensor + temp → SMS/app alerts.  
**Revenue:** Sell monitoring kit (₱3-5k per pond vs ₱15k+ commercial).  
**ABE edge:** Aquacultural engineering.

### B4. Poultry House Environment Monitor
**Problem:** Backyard poultry farmers lose birds to heat stress (common in Mindoro).  
**Solution:** ESP32 + temp/humidity + ammonia sensor → alerts + fan control.  
**Revenue:** Sensor kit for backyard to small-scale poultry raisers.  
**ABE edge:** Agricultural structures, animal environment.

## Category C: Agri Supply Chain & Marketplace

### C1. Farm-to-Market Price Tracker (Mindoro)
**Problem:** Farmers don't know real-time market prices → middlemen exploit information gap.  
**Solution:** Mobile app crowdsourcing prices from public markets + DA price data. Mindoro-focused.  
**Revenue:** Ads, premium alerts for price thresholds, sponsored listings for buyers.  
**ABE edge:** Moderate — agri domain knowledge.

### C2. Harvest Scheduling & Logistics App
**Problem:** Small farmers can't coordinate harvest timing with available trucking/jeepney.  
**Solution:** App matching farmers with available transport + optimal harvest date suggestions.  
**Revenue:** Commission on transport bookings, premium for harvest prediction.  
**ABE edge:** Farm management, post-harvest logistics.

### C3. Agricultural Input Group-Buy Platform
**Problem:** Smallholders pay retail for seeds, fertilizer, pesticide. Bulk prices need volume.  
**Solution:** App for farmers to pool orders → negotiate bulk discounts from suppliers.  
**Revenue:** Commission on group purchases (5-10%).  
**ABE edge:** Moderate — agri supply chain.

## Category D: Rural Micro-Business Tools

### D1. Sari-Sari Store Inventory + Credit Tracker
**Problem:** Sari-sari stores track "utang" (credit) on paper → losses, disputes.  
**Solution:** Simple mobile app: scan/type products, track credit per customer, reorder alerts.  
**Revenue:** Freemium (free basic, paid for analytics/supplier connections).  
**ABE edge:** Low — but huge market (1M+ stores).

### D2. Micro-Livestock Record Keeper
**Problem:** Backyard livestock raisers (goat, pig, chicken) keep no records → can't track ROI.  
**Solution:** Mobile app: animal inventory, feed cost tracking, breeding records, profit calculator.  
**Revenue:** Freemium + premium vet consultation referrals.  
**ABE edge:** Good — animal production systems.

### D3. Farm Financial Record App (Filipino-First)
**Problem:** Filipino smallholder farmers keep zero financial records → can't get loans or track profit.  
**Solution:** Ultra-simple app in Filipino/Tagalog: input costs, harvests, sales. Auto-computes profit per crop cycle.  
**Revenue:** Partner with rural banks/microfinance for loan facilitation (referral fee).  
**ABE edge:** Farm management economics.

## Category E: Climate, Water & Soil

### E1. Rainfall Logger for Smallholders
**Problem:** No hyperlocal rain data in rural areas → bad planting decisions.  
**Solution:** Cheap rain gauge + ESP32 + solar panel → logs rainfall, sends to phone.  
**Revenue:** Sell logger units, aggregate data for agricultural insurance companies.  
**ABE edge:** Hydrology, land-water management.

### E2. Soil Health Kit + App
**Problem:** Farmers guess soil needs → over/under-fertilize.  
**Solution:** Low-cost soil test kit (pH, NPK colorimetric strips) + smartphone app to photograph strips → AI reads color → fertilizer recommendation.  
**Revenue:** Sell test strip refills (consumable model) + app subscription.  
**ABE edge:** Soil and water conservation, crop science interface.

### E3. Flood Early Warning (Barangay-Level)
**Problem:** Mindoro rivers flood during typhoons, little warning for riverside barangays.  
**Solution:** Ultrasonic water level sensor + ESP32 + solar → SMS blast to barangay officials when threshold hit.  
**Revenue:** LGU contracts (DRRM funds), NGO funding.  
**ABE edge:** Hydrology, watershed management.

## Category F: Agri-Education & Services

### F1. AR Pest/Disease Identifier
**Problem:** Farmers misidentify pests → wrong pesticide → money wasted + health risk.  
**Solution:** Phone camera app: point at pest/disease symptom → ML identifies + recommends IPM action.  
**Revenue:** Freemium + agrochemical company sponsorship for product recs.  
**ABE edge:** Crop protection, IPM.

### F2. Farm Equipment Rental Marketplace
**Problem:** Smallholders can't afford their own hand tractor, thresher, etc.  
**Solution:** App connecting equipment owners with renters. GPS tracking for accountability.  
**Revenue:** Commission per rental transaction.  
**ABE edge:** Strong — farm machinery/power is core ABE.

### F3. GAP/Organic Certification Checklist App
**Problem:** Farmers want Good Agricultural Practices certification but process is confusing.  
**Solution:** Step-by-step digital checklist, document organizer, progress tracker for DA-BAI GAP certification.  
**Revenue:** Subscription for farmers, bulk license for cooperatives/LGUs.  
**ABE edge:** Good — farm management standards.

## Category G: Wild Cards

### G1. Vermiculture Monitoring System
**Problem:** Vermicompost producers monitor bins manually — too wet/dry/hot kills worms.  
**Solution:** ESP32 + moisture + temp sensors in vermibed → alerts.  
**Revenue:** Sell kits to vermicompost operators (growing market with organic farming push).  
**ABE edge:** Solid waste management, organic agriculture.

### G2. Bamboo/Wood Moisture Meter (For Farmers Building Structures)
**Problem:** Farmers build with unseasoned bamboo/wood → structures fail.  
**Solution:** Cheap resistive moisture meter (Arduino + probes) with go/no-go indicator.  
**Revenue:** Sell device at hardware stores, agri-supply shops.  
**ABE edge:** Agricultural structures, materials.

### G3. Smart Composting Bin (Community Scale)
**Problem:** Barangay composting mandated by RA 9003 but bins poorly managed.  
**Solution:** Temp + moisture monitored compost bin with turning reminders via app.  
**Revenue:** Sell to LGUs, schools, subdivisions.  
**ABE edge:** Waste management, soil science.

### G4. Mushroom Growing Chamber Controller
**Problem:** Oyster mushroom growing booming in PH but environment control is manual.  
**Solution:** ESP32 + humidity/temp control for growing chamber. Auto-mist, auto-vent.  
**Revenue:** Kit sales to mushroom growers (fast-growing micro-enterprise segment).  
**ABE edge:** Controlled environment, post-harvest.
